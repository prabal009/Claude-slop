10. DYNAMIC QUEUE ETA ENGINE
Implement or consume deterministic calculations that match the Student Portal.

Demonstration assumptions
Job setup time: 1 minute.

Black-and-white processing: 0.4 minutes per printed side.

Colour processing: 0.8 minutes per printed side.

Formula:

printedSides = pageCount × copies

jobDuration = 1 + printedSides × processingTimePerSide

For queued jobs, estimate waiting time based on unfinished jobs ahead in queue order, including remaining processing work from the currently active job.

Distinguish:

Time before printing begins.

Own job processing duration.

Estimated time until ready for pickup.

For example:

If three preceding jobs require 4, 5, and 6 minutes, the estimated wait before printing starts is 15 minutes, ignoring any additional current-job work.

Recalculate when:

Jobs arrive.

Jobs are reordered.

Statuses change.

Urgent requests are approved.

Printer availability changes.

Printing configuration changes.

Do not use random ETAs.

When the shared queue engine is available, treat its output as authoritative.

Do not maintain a separate conflicting calculation implementation.
